# Enzyme supplement FODZYME: a fall in IBS symptoms in a study without a placebo group

A study from the University of Michigan, presented at the congress of the American College of Gastroenterology, describes a fall in IBS symptoms after four weeks of using the enzyme supplement FODZYME. This is not proof that the product works for everyone, and it is not a recommendation to buy or use it.

> This is general information and not personal medical advice.

## What was studied?

442 adults with IBS added an enzyme mixture to their food for four weeks (fructan hydrolase, alpha-galactosidase and lactase). Almost half (46,6%) were already following a low-FODMAP diet. There was no placebo group: everyone received the same supplement.

## What were the findings?

In 69,2% of the participants the symptom score (IBS-SSS) fell. More than 40% saw an improvement in abdominal pain and in bloating. Satisfaction with the diet also rose on average. The manufacturer Kiwi Biosciences paid for the study. Several authors have financial ties to this company.

## What this does and does not mean

Without a placebo group it is not possible to say whether the fall comes from the supplement, from expectation, or because people changed their diet in another way. The result was presented as a congress abstract and a press release, not as a fully peer-reviewed journal article. This article is not product advice.

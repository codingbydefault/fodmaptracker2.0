# A calmer autumn routine

Shorter days and a changing weekly rhythm can alter familiar habits. A diary is easiest to maintain when it takes little attention while preserving useful context.

## Choose one regular logging moment

Log straight after a meal or during a set evening moment. Record what you ate, the approximate portion and only the context that may genuinely help later. Your record does not have to be perfect.

## Separate facts from interpretation

First capture what happened: meal, time and any symptom. A pattern only becomes meaningful when it recurs. One day or one meal does not prove a cause.

## End the week calmly

Review your entries once a week. Look not only for symptoms, but also for days that went well. Discuss major dietary changes or persistent symptoms with a doctor or FODMAP-trained dietitian.

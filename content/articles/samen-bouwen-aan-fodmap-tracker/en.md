# Building Fodmap-Tracker together

A simpler button, clearer wording or a better weekly overview can make a real difference. We therefore collect general experiences and look for recurring usability problems.

## One response is a signal

An idea is valuable, but it does not automatically represent every user. We compare feedback, review risks and test on a small scale before anything becomes a permanent feature.

## Do not share medical details publicly

Feel free to describe which action is difficult, but do not post diagnoses, symptom histories, medication or identifiable health information on social media. Use only the secure areas of the app or discuss it with your healthcare professional.

## From feedback to improvement

We assess which problem is being solved, for whom, the work involved and any privacy or health risks. Available features, tests and future ideas remain clearly separated.

Thank you for helping make Fodmap-Tracker calmer, clearer and more useful.

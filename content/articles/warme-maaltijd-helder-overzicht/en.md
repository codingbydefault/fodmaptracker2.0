# Warm meal, clear overview

A soup, bake or stew often contains several ingredients. A useful entry still does not need to be lengthy.

## Start with the meal as you know it

Give the dish a recognisable name and record an approximate amount. Add only ingredients you may want to find later. For packaged foods, the label checker can flag additional points for attention.

## Add context when it matters

Consider the mealtime, an unusual portion or an ingredient you are deliberately testing during reintroduction. Avoid guessing afterwards; leave unknown details empty.

## Record symptoms separately

A symptom can be linked to a meal, but this does not automatically make the meal its cause. Review multiple records and take personal conclusions to a qualified healthcare professional.

> Ingredient suitability depends on portion and product. Use the app as a tool, not as a product test or personal dietary advice.
